## Add mods

Use the mod search on the edit page to find Hytale mods. Add the ones you want, then save.

## Export

From the modpack detail page, use **Export** to download a ZIP you can drop into your game or server folder.

## Share

Set visibility to **Public** to list your pack on Explore for others to discover and like.
