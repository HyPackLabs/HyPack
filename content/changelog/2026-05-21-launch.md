## Modpack builder

- Create, edit, and delete modpacks from **My modpacks**
- Search and add Hytale mods from the CurseForge API
- Set visibility to **Private**, **Unlisted**, or **Public**
- Upload a custom modpack icon (JPEG or PNG, max 2 MB)
- Export a ZIP with mod files, generated `config.json`, and a README
- Duplicate your own packs or fork public and unlisted packs from other creators

## Discover and share

- **Explore** — browse and search public modpacks
- **Likes** — save favorites to **My likes**
- Creator profile pages at `/users/[userId]`

## Accounts

- Sign in and sign up with Clerk (email and social providers configured in your Clerk dashboard)
- Protected routes for creating and managing modpacks

## Site content

- **Blog** — markdown posts in `content/blog/`
- **Changelog** — release notes in `content/changelog/`
- **Guides** — markdown docs in `content/guides/`, with subfolders as categories in the nav dropdown

## Homepage

- Hero, live Hytale mod count from CurseForge, and featured mods
- Featured modpacks from the HyPack community account
